Introduction

Build your vocabulary list, check the dictionary for words that you don't know and practice your Mandarin!

The Zi application let's you do all that all together.

To get it working properly, you will need to be able to type Chinese on your Android device. If you have not done so already, it is suggested you install the Google Pinyin Input to do that.

Adding vocabulary

The first thing you want to do is to be able to edit your vocabulary list. As you type a chinese word you want to add, you can quickly add translations coming from the dictionary or you can add your own.

You can manage your list, add a new one, delete existing ones (it will not delete the vocabulary it contains though), merge them, clone them... if you set one as the default list, any vocabulary you will add in this session will be automatically assigned to that lesson.

By selecting the lists, you can review the vocabulary it contains.

Testing

As you are studying and reviewing your vocabulary, you will go through different tests to make sure you do not forget what you have learnt.

The different tests are:

  • Guess the tone of a character
  • Guess a missing character within one or multiple words
  • Guess the Chinese translation of an English word
  • Guess the English translation of a Chinese word
  • Guess the pinyin from a character

In some cases, you can tell the engine your answer is actually correct even if it does not match exactly what is in your list (but is a close synonym)

The application will keep track of your progress and show you the statistics of how many words your have learnt, how many characters and as you continue your studying, it will show you a graph of your progress

Dictionary

In the dictionary, you can query some Chinese words, either in Chinese characters, in pinyins with or without tones or a mixture of each. For example, you may search for "你好", "nihao", "ni3hao3" or even "你hao"

You can do this search in the dictionary or within your vocabulary list.

When you click on a word, you can see more details about it, with translations and any other words or sentences that may contain that word. You can "pin" that page to make sure it stays there as you are doing other searches.
