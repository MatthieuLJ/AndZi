Tests

When you are being tested on your current vocabulary list to best studied, the kind of test will be randomly picked every time. The following screens will give more information for each of the different tests.

Depending on your settings, you can elect to see the details about the character or word you were tested on after the test or just see a message whether you were right or wrong. In some cases when the application cannot tell if the answer is correct or not, you may be able to force it to accept the answer.
